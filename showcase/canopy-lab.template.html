The 75. Three new directions.

Three real 3D canopy studies on the same Rift 75. Drag each view to rotate. These are design proposals; the current flight model stays unchanged.

A / SHELL

Rounded shell

A compact, smooth nose around the lens and a rounded body tapering towards the antenna.

Enclosed camera · Side cooling slots · Rounded lens opening

B / CAGE

Open rib cage

A light, open structure with slender arches, visible electronics and a separate camera surround.

Open sides · Minimal plastic · Air-style structure

C / VISOR

Low racing visor

A low swept roof, a compact camera face and open rear supports, with a small orange accent.

Low silhouette · Open rear · Sportier nose

Design study only. All three use the same camera position, frame, motors and battery. No changes to flight physics.
